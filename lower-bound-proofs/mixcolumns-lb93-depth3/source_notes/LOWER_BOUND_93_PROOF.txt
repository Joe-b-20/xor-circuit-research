AES FORWARD MIXCOLUMNS: AT LEAST 93 XOR GATES AT DEPTH THREE
2026-10-17

THEOREM AND SCOPE

Let D3 be the minimum number of two-input XOR gates computing the 32-bit
AES forward MixColumns map, with raw inputs at depth zero and all gates at
depth at most three. Arbitrary sharing, fan-out, cancellation, and asymmetric
circuits are permitted. Then

                            93 <= D3 <= 97.

The upper bound is the actual circuit control_97.json, rechecked on all 32
output masks and all gate depths. The lower bound is computer-assisted.
This does not determine which of 93, 94, 95, 96, or 97 is optimal.

1. THE LOSSLESS REDUCTION

The earlier normal-form argument removes redundant gates and represents a
minimum circuit by P distinct pair helpers, T triple helpers, Q quartet
helpers, and 32 output gates. Output rows have weight five or seven, so none
can be an internal gate of depth at most two. Put H=P+T+Q and G=32+H. Pairs
can be computed directly from raw inputs; triples are pair+input, and
quartets are sums of disjoint pairs. The complete output recipes retain all
permitted cancellation, including a foreign cancelling input for a
weight-five output.

The previously verified inequalities are

                     P >= 26,       4H-P >= 214.

If G<=92, then H<=60. These inequalities force H=60 and P=26, hence T+Q=34.
See PAIR26_PROOF.txt and PAIR_TRADEOFF_PROOF.txt. The tradeoff is proved by
exact integer arithmetic, including the full correction for rounded dual
coefficients; it is not an optimizer's floating-point assertion.

2. ODD/EVEN PARITY GIVES THE COMPONENT CASES

Build the pair graph on the 32 raw inputs, with an edge for every pair
helper. Let c be its component count and beta its cycle rank. Then
beta=P-32+c. The previously established bound is c<=8. At P=26 this leaves

                   (c,beta) = (6,0), (7,1), or (8,2).

Every odd output unfolds as one raw-input anchor plus pair signals. An
edge contributes even parity inside its component, so each output
intersects exactly one component oddly. If M is the MixColumns binary
matrix and u is a component indicator, (u,Mu) is a graph-code word. All
component words partition the 64 input/output coordinates. Every nonzero
such word has even weight at least six.

The eight-component branch was already excluded at 92 gates: P=26,c=8
requires T+Q>=38, hence G>=96. See EIGHT_COMPONENT_PROOF.txt and its complete
checker verify_eight_component_tradeoff.py.

3. NEW CONDITIONAL COMPONENT INEQUALITIES

For an actual pair component C, all crossing pairs are absent. A triple
or quartet is absent if no depth-two construction using internal pairs
exists. Every recipe using an absent helper is absent. Connectivity also
forces at least |C|-1 internal edges and at least one edge across every
proper nonempty internal cut.

Add these restrictions and P<=26 to the previously verified 16,868-row
system. Seventeen rational certificates show that their component shapes
force H>60. Their byte rotations exclude 68 of the 84 weight-six graph-code
words. The remaining sixteen are isolated input bits at positions 0, 2,
3, or 6 in any of the four bytes.

verify_component_conditionals.py independently reconstructs all restrictions,
connectivity rows, and exact dual corrections. All seventeen certificates
passed. Rotation symmetry of the actual matrix is checked. Rotation reuses
conditional proofs; it imposes no symmetry on an unknown circuit.

4. COMPLETE PARTITION ENUMERATION

Sort component words by (weight, numeric mask). For six components the
first four have weight at most fourteen: if the fourth had weight at least
sixteen, the total would be at least 3*6+3*16>64. For seven components the
first five have weight at most twelve, since 4*6+3*14>64. Generate every
required short word from M and its inverse. Enumerate disjoint initial
words; find ALL last-two-word splits by exact binary kernels, without a
size bound on the last two words.

Weight-seven outputs meeting a component in exactly two inputs force the
pair joining them. The forced graph's cycle rank is a lower bound on the
component's cycle rank. A certified local forest obstruction supplies
another lower bound of one. Taking the maximum of these two bounds per
component and summing across components is valid. Retain every partition
with total lower-bound tax at most beta. These necessary filters cannot
remove an actual circuit.

The next-word average-weight restriction applies ONLY to the next word,
not to later, possibly heavier words. This is the corrected rule described
in ENUMERATION_CORRECTION.txt.

6. THE EXACT FARKAS CONTRADICTION

Fix an actual component partition. Write all regenerated valid rows as
Av>=b, with 0<=v_j<=1. Append P<=26, H<=60, and at least |C|-1 internal
pair edges for each component C. Let J be the variables not ruled out by
the partition; v_j=0 for j outside J. Each certificate gives nonnegative
INTEGER row multipliers u.

For every feasible v,

          u*b <= (A^T u)*v <= sum_(j in J) max(0,(A^T u)_j).

The exact checker verifies the STRICT OPPOSITE inequality for each of the
909 six-component and 204 seven-component representatives. Therefore none
admits such a circuit. A positive stored denominator only scales the
multipliers; the contradiction is checked entirely with integers. No
optimizer status or numerical tolerance is a proof premise.

verify_partition_conditionals.py imports neither the discovery LP nor an
optimizer. It independently reconstructs availability, connectivity, and
integer coefficients, then checks every certificate. All 1,113 passed.
