An exact unrestricted depth-three lower bound of 86 XOR gates
2026-10-16

RESULT

For one AES forward MixColumns column, using two-input XOR gates with
inputs at depth zero, arbitrary sharing, and arbitrary cancellation,

                         86 <= D3 <= 97.

The upper bound is the included 97-gate circuit. The lower bound is an
exact rational certificate over a complete circuit relaxation. This
does not determine the exact full-circuit optimum.

The twelve weight-seven outputs alone have exact complexity 48 at
depth three. That separate exact result supplies new valid inequalities
for the full problem. It is not a claim that the full optimum can be
obtained by independently optimizing output clusters. In fact, the
known97 circuit's weight-seven ancestry has 55 gates, showing why
additional local work can be useful for sharing with the other outputs.

1. COMPLETE CIRCUIT MODEL

PROOF.md sections 2-4 give the normalization and completeness proofs.
They are unchanged by this extension. There are 32 final output gates
and helper gates of weights two, three, four. A normalized circuit has

                         G=32+P+T+Q.

All possible helpers and all possible complete output constructions
are retained: 496 pairs, 3008 triples, 4889 quartets, and 53,280 output
recipes. The twenty weight-five outputs retain all cancelling 3+4
decompositions. No no-cancellation assumption is made for them.

An actual circuit chooses one recipe per output. With binary helper
variables x_h and recipe variables z_(o,r), use the valid constraints

  sum_r z_(o,r) >= 1,
  x_h >= sum_(r containing h) z_(o,r)  for each output/helper pair,
  sum_(pairs h) x_h >= 24,
  0<=x_h,z_(o,r)<=1.

The parity premise P>=24 is proved in PROOF.md section 3 and checked
by verify_pair_rank.py. The original model also includes the valid
fifteen-parent inequality for the weight-seven outputs.

2. THE 43 NEW INEQUALITIES

Let U_b be the complete helper universe of the four weight-seven
outputs at bit b. The corresponding selected helpers contain the actual
ancestry of those outputs, so any nonnegative-cost lower bound for a
local ancestry also holds for the selected helpers in U_b.

For each b in {1,3,4}, add:
  - at least twelve helpers (the exact sixteen-gate local theorem);
  - at least six pairs and at least five triple/quartet parents;
  - at least twelve helpers after assigning cost zero to pairs entirely
    in either one of the two side families, separately.

These are fifteen inequalities. BOUNDARY_TAX_PROOF.txt proves them.

For each of the three pairs of clusters, add at least twenty-four
helpers in the union of their universes. Add at least thirty-six
helpers in the union of all three universes. These four inequalities
follow from the exact 32- and 48-gate results in the two cluster notes.

Finally add twenty-four weighted local inequalities. The exact weighted
helper minima are 115 and 116 with charges scaled by ten, as checked
in weighted_cluster_certificate.py. Each of the two inequalities can
be mapped to any of the three isomorphic clusters. The canonical
four-output problem also admits the reflection

  a_i -> a_(-i), b_i -> b_(1-i), c_i -> c_(1-i)

and swapping b and c. These permutations preserve the four-output
problem and transfer every weighted inequality. Two weighted patterns,
three clusters, two reflection choices, and two swap choices give
twenty-four valid inequalities. Coefficients remain nonnegative.

Thus the strengthened relaxation has 15,169 rows and 61,673 variables.
The checker independently regenerates the original recipes and all
43 new rows; it does not import the new model's row-generation code.

3. EXACT RATIONAL CERTIFICATE

Write the rows as A*p>=b, with 0<=p<=1 and objective c*p=P+T+Q.
For any nonnegative row multiplier vector u, let

  e_j=max(0,(A^T*u)_j-c_j).

Then c*p >= u*b-sum_j e_j. Indeed, (A^T*u-c)*p<=sum_j e_j follows
coordinatewise from 0<=p_j<=1, and u*A*p>=u*b follows from u>=0.

certificate_86.json records nonnegative integer numerators for u with
denominator 10^12. All checking arithmetic is integer arithmetic.
Rounding of the numerical multipliers is repaired by subtracting every
positive excess explicitly. The exact checked values are

  raw numerator       = 53,307,334,336,938
  repair numerator    =              2,779
  certified numerator = 53,307,334,334,159
  denominator         =  1,000,000,000,000.

Consequently

  P+T+Q >= 53.307334334159 > 53,

so the integer number of helpers is at least 54. Therefore

                         G>=32+54=86.

The numerical optimizer only discovers multipliers. Its status and
floating-point objective are not premises of this lower-bound proof.
independent_check_86.py reconstructs every row and checks the complete
integer calculation. It also verifies that the known97 construction
satisfies all the old and new inequalities.

4. WHAT IS STILL MISSING

To prove the 97-gate construction optimal, one must exclude every
integral selection with at most 64 helpers. The new certificate excludes
only selections with at most 53 helpers. Exact optimality of the
weight-seven subsystem does not by itself control all sharing with
the twenty weight-five outputs, whose allowed cancellations remain
present in the complete model.

The earlier 31-gate cluster3/4 CP-SAT run returned UNKNOWN and is not
used as evidence. The later exhaustive rational charging certificate
is what proves that subsystem's 32-gate optimum. All failed charging
conjectures were discarded before forming this certificate.
