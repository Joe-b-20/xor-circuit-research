PROOF OF (1)

Use precisely the rows and finite premises of the independently verified
certificate_depth3_linked_92.json. There are 16,868 valid rows A v >= b,
61,673 variables, and bounds 0<=v_j<=1. The original helper objective is
c v=H. All actual normalized circuits have integral v, with one actual
complete recipe chosen for each output.

The existing certificate has denominator D=10^16, nonnegative integer
row multipliers u_i, and exact bounded-variable correction

  R = sum_j max(0, (A^T u)_j - D*c_j).

It proves H >= (u*b-R)/D = 597778328006951391/10^16.

Row 15255 is the pair-count inequality P>=26. Its multiplier is

                         lambda = 2499973710473077.

Multiply all row multipliers by four and set the multiplier of row 15255
to zero. The resulting multipliers are still nonnegative. Use the new
integer objective c'v=3P+4T+4Q: coefficient 3 on pair helpers, 4 on triple
and quartet helpers, and 0 on recipe-selection variables. Recompute the
bounded-variable correction against this objective, using exact integers.
The independently regenerated calculation gives

  raw numerator:         2131495970626858740
  correction numerator:      432069609681296
  corrected numerator:   2131063901017177444
  denominator:                    10^16.

Therefore

  3P+4T+4Q >= 213.1063901017177444 > 213.

The left side is an integer, proving (1).

The correction is essential: it is subtracted in full, with no appeal to
floating-point feasibility or an optimizer's reported status. No additional
finite-enumeration result is needed beyond the prior verified certificate.

PROOF OF THE 92-GATE REDUCTION

Since 3P+4T+4Q=4H-P, (1) gives 4H-P>=214. If G=92 then H=60, so
P<=240-214=26. The established pair lower bound P>=26 forces P=26,
and hence T+Q=34.

An additional unrounded consequence of the same source multipliers is

  H >= 59.7778328006951391 + 0.2499973710473077*(P-26).

For P>=27 its right side is at least 60.0278301717424468. Thus H>=61
by integrality, giving G>=93 directly as well.
