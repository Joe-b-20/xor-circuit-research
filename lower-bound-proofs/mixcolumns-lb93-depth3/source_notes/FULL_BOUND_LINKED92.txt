MODEL AND RESULT

Two-input XOR gates, raw inputs at depth zero, output depth at most three,
unrestricted sharing, fan-out, and cancellation. The full optimum remains
unresolved: 92 <= D3 <= 97. No smaller full circuit was found this turn.
The exact minimum normalized pair count remains 26, witnessed by the
previous verified 100-gate circuit. That is not a 100-gate full optimum.

NEW VALID INEQUALITIES

LINKED_CLUSTER_PROOF.txt proves inequalities that price both the union of
helpers and the actual complete recipe selected for each output. Helper
prices are nonnegative; recipe prices may be signed. Their right-hand sides
come from complete integer enumeration of all four-output recipe tuples.
The weight-five case projects all global recipes onto the cluster support;
foreign cancellation is included, and every projection is checked.

EDGE_TREE_PROOF.txt refines the parent forest inequalities by assigning a
separate reward to each exact parent edge (a,b,output), rather than only
to its output label. Each retained price is checked against every legal
connected tree with distinct output labels in its stated vertex set.

CANONICAL_PARENT_PROOF.txt separately gives cost-preserving private-parent
replacement rules. These rules are NOT premises of the new certificate.
They apply to minimum-total-gate representatives and do not preserve the
pair count. Their numerical improvement was negligible.

LIMITS OF THIS CHECKPOINT

The new inequalities make local helper purchases agree more closely with
actual recipe choices, but do not force globally integral choices of
individual masks. Counts 92 through 96 remain unexcluded. There is no
claim that 97 is optimal, or that every possible inequality of the new
types has been exhausted. Separate exploratory integer-count and circuit
search records must not be interpreted as proofs of infeasibility.
