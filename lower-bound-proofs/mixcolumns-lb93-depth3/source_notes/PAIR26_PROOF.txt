AES forward MixColumns, depth at most three: P >= 26
2026-10-16; enumeration corrected and reverified 2026-10-17

MODEL

Two-input XOR gates, raw inputs at depth zero, arbitrary sharing, fan-out,
and cancellation. The lossless normalization in PROOF.md gives distinct
pair signals at depth one, triples and quartets at depth two, and 32 output
gates at depth three. Let P count the normalized pairs. The result is P>=26.
This does not assert that P=26 is attainable or determine the full optimum.

1. WHY ONLY TWO CASES NEED EXCLUDING

The graph of pair signals has 32 vertices, P edges, and c components. Its
cycle rank is beta=P-32+c. The parity argument shows that each output meets
exactly one component oddly. Thus the component words (u,M*u) partition
the 64 input/output coordinates. The previously proved finite graph-code
lemma gives c<=8; verify_pair_rank.py independently rechecks this lemma.

If P<=25, then c>=32-P>=7. Hence either:

  c=7, beta=0: every component is a tree;
  c=8, beta<=1: at most one independent cycle in the entire graph.

These two cases cover P<=25, including P=24. No extra symmetry or restriction
on circuit topology has been imposed.

2. A COMPLETE NECESSARY PAIR-RECIPE LIST

Forget the cost of triples and quartets and retain only pair signals needed
by an output. Superset pair recipes can be discarded: if a smaller recipe
is available, the pair-only feasibility question is already satisfied.
The following lists are complete, including cancellation.

For a weight-seven output Y, choose its raw-input anchor a in Y and a perfect
matching of the six remaining bits. These are the 7*15=105 pair recipes.
The unfolded ancestry has seven distinct leaves, so there is no cancellation.

For a weight-five output Y, the minimal recipes are:

  (i) Choose an anchor a in Y and match the other four bits into two pairs.
      This gives 5*3=15 recipes.

  (ii) Choose an anchor a in Y, two bits b,c among the remaining four, and
       a foreign bit z outside Y. Use pairs {b,z}, {c,z}, and the pair of
       the other two output bits. This gives 5*6*27=810 recipes.

To see completeness, the final-parent cases are 1+4, disjoint 3+2, or
overlapping 3+4 with one cancelled foreign bit z. The first two give (i).
In the cancelling case, write the triple as an anchor plus a pair, and
the quartet as two disjoint pairs. If the anchor is z, the three pairs
form a matching of Y union {z}; dropping the pair containing z leaves a
recipe of type (i). Otherwise the two pairs containing z form a path
through z, and the third pair is disjoint, giving (ii). Conversely these
recipes can be realized at depth three: for (ii), form the triple from
{b,z} plus anchor a, and the quartet from {c,z} and the remaining pair.

The independent checker generates these 105/825 lists directly, without
using the complete helper-model recipe generator. They agree with its
pair projections after superset removal. Their validity is unrestricted:
all 27 possible foreign cancellation bits are included for every Y.

3. LOCAL FOREST OBSTRUCTIONS

For a proposed pair-graph component C, no selected pair may cross C. For
each output recipe, discard it if it uses a crossing pair, then retain
only its pairs lying inside C. The outside pairs are deliberately ignored.
Every actual circuit with C as a component must satisfy the resulting
local requirements. This is a relaxation, so local infeasibility excludes C.

The checker searches exactly for an acyclic set of internal pairs supporting
at least one projected recipe for every output. It only prunes when:

  - adding a recipe exceeds the allowed cycle rank;
  - an output has no remaining viable recipe; or
  - the same already-proved-infeasible edge set is revisited.

An edge in every viable recipe of an unsatisfied output is forced. Otherwise
the search branches over every viable recipe of one unsatisfied output.
Cycle rank cannot decrease when edges are added. These rules are exhaustive.
Search limits produce INCOMPLETE, never a proof of exclusion.

forest_obstruction_cover.txt contains 4,082 component representatives. The
checker rejects every one as a forest, with 1,348,913 total search nodes.
Only rejected components are used. The byte rotation of AES maps every
output to another output and preserves pair constructions; the checker
verifies this permutation of the actual AES rows. Thus every rotation of
a certified component is also a forest obstruction.

For any C, define a lower bound on its necessary cycle rank:

  t(C)=max(beta(F(C)), 1 if C is a certified forest obstruction, else 0),

where F(C) consists of the forced pair edges C intersect Y whenever a
weight-seven output Y meets C in exactly two inputs. The forced-edge
argument is proved in PAIR25_PROOF.txt. Taking a maximum, rather than a
sum, avoids double-counting the same cycle. Across different components,
these cycle-rank lower bounds may be added.

4. INDEPENDENT COMPLETE PARTITION ENUMERATION

Every nonzero graph-code word (u,M*u) has even weight at least six. All
words of weight at most twelve are independently regenerated by enumerating
input and output halves of weight at most six, using M and its inverse.
At least one half of any such word has weight at most six. The counts are:

  total weight:       6      8      10      12
  number of words:  84    276    1300    8112.

For seven sorted words partitioning 64 coordinates, the first five have
weight at most twelve: otherwise 4*6+3*14>64. For eight sorted words, the
first six have weight at most ten: otherwise 5*6+3*12>64.

The Python verifier independently enumerates these sorted disjoint short
words, respecting the total cycle budget. For the remaining support R with
input half I and output half O, it solves over GF(2)

                M[rows outside O, columns in I] * v = 0.

Every solution is a graph-code word supported inside R. The verifier lists
every vector of this exact kernel and tests each proper word with its
complement in R. This exhausts the final two blocks, even when their
weights exceed the short-word cutoff. It does not read the discovery
partition files or assume their counts.

Seven components, cycle budget zero:
  short words after filtering:  5,486
  search nodes:                 8,562,660
  five-word leaves:             2,936,998
  distinct remainders:            695,385
  surviving partitions:                 0.

Eight components, cycle budget one:
  short words after filtering:  1,600
  search nodes:                 6,566,068
  six-word leaves:              1,225,018
  distinct remainders:            145,332
  surviving partitions:             2,848.

For each of those 2,848 partitions, the complete pair-recipe search allows
every internal pair and all 105/825 output recipes consistent with the
partition. It rejects all 2,848 with cycle budget one, in 111,364 search nodes.
There are no timeouts or unresolved cases in the verification.

Both possible cases for P<=25 are therefore impossible. Hence P>=26.

5. VERIFICATION AND SCOPE

  python verify_pair26.py

Requires standard-library Python and g++ with C++17. The default invocation
also rechecks the earlier c<=8 premise. The 97-gate circuit is reconstructed
and all of its outputs and depths are checked; its 27 pair signals satisfy
every regenerated output-recipe requirement.

This is a computer-assisted proof with explicit mathematical reductions and
complete finite checking. It is not a proof-assistant formalization. No
optimization solver's infeasibility status is used as a premise. P>=26
alone is not a claim of 26-pair attainability or full circuit optimality.
