\vspace{11pt}
{\large Joe Bachir}\par
{\sffamily\small\color{muted} Review edition \textbullet\ 23 October 2026}\par
\begin{statementbox}
{\sffamily\bfseries\color{accent} THE LOWER-BOUND STATEMENT}\par
\vspace{3pt}
{\Large $L(M)\ge 75$}\par
Every binary XOR circuit computing one 32-bit column of AES forward MixColumns needs at least 75 gates. Depth, fan-out, intermediate masks, cancellation, and output reuse are unrestricted.
\end{statementbox}
\textbf{Proof route.} The proof combines an even-span partition bound, an odd-signal graph, exact transposition and a bounded potential. Necessary inequalities on helper types then exclude the remaining 73- and 74-gate profiles using exact integer certificates.\par
\textbf{Fresh verification.} All fourteen finite premises were recomputed. Both certificate implementations accepted 484 certificates for the 73-gate exclusion and 4,057 for the 74-gate exclusion. The inherited stress checks passed on thirteen control circuits. The accompanying 88-gate witness was rechecked.\par
\textbf{Reading and reproduction.} Read the proof below, then use the package's \texttt{README.md} for the file map and environment details. From the extracted package folder, run:\par
\vspace{3pt}
{\ttfamily\small python3 verify\_all.py}\par
\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{Scope of the result.} The included construction gives $75\le L(M)\le88$. This package does not establish the exact minimum.\par
\textbf{Source and review status.} The full argument follows the public review draft 1.1 of 2 October 2026. The original source and its identity are preserved; local exposition and presentation changes are listed separately. The work was developed with AI assistance and is submitted for expert review. Exact computational checks are documented; the mathematical argument is not proof-assistant formalized.\par
