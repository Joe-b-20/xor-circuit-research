HELPER--HELPER EQUATIONS MUST PAY FOR THE THREE MISSING BOUNDARY DIMENSIONS
23 October 2026.  New push6 theorem; push5 remains unchanged.

SCOPE AND PRINCIPAL RESULT

These are necessary conditions for every normalized acyclic two-input XOR
circuit computing AES MixColumns TRANSPOSE.  All nonzero signal masks,
parent choices, depths, cancellation, fanout, and output reuse are allowed.
Nothing assumes that an aggregate feasible point is an executable circuit.

Let Q_* be the missing even labels that are NOT repeated-bright.  If Q_*
is empty, then

                    z_U - h_00 >= 3.                         (T)

Here z_U is the number of helper--helper (HH) equations with at least one
unanchored helper endpoint, and h_00 counts helpers having no helper--
boundary (HB) equation at all.  Consequently z-h_00>=3 as well, where z
counts every HH equation.

In particular, z<=2 forces at least one exceptional missing label.  This
excludes all three displayed transpose profiles in the frozen push5
EXACT_REMAINING_RELAXATION_POINTS.json.  It does not by itself exclude
all other aggregate profiles or improve the numerical global gate bound.

1. DEFINITIONS AND INHERITED EXACT-BOUNDARY FACTS

The boundary B consists of the 32 raw inputs and 32 required outputs of
M^T.  These are distinct odd values.  Its formal evaluation map has rank
32; therefore its complete relation space has dimension 64-32=32.
Let R10 be the span of its true relations with support at most ten.
The complete fixed-boundary census gives dim(R10)=29.

Normalize to distinct nonzero signal values.  Each gate contributes its
three-signal defining equation.  All defining rows are independent: the
latest gate result in a proposed dependence cannot cancel.  Since raw
inputs are present, these rows span the full formal evaluation kernel.
Eliminating all nonboundary signal coordinates must leave the complete
32-dimensional boundary relation space.

In the odd-signal graph, each OO or OE gate equation is an edge joining
its two odd signals, labeled by its even signal.  A nonoutput odd signal
is a helper.  An even label is represented if an ACTUAL boundary--boundary
(BB) defining edge exists.  The other q even labels are missing.

At a helper H, r counts its represented-label HB edges, u its missing-label
HB edges, and v its HH edges.  It is anchored if r>0.  A missing label is
repeated-bright if an actual HB edge in that label touches a helper with
r>=2.  Q_* is the set of missing labels that fail this property.

Let h_00=#{H:r=u=0}, V0=sum_(r=0)v, and z_U be the number of HH edges
touching at least one helper with r=0.  Always z_U<=z and z_U<=V0.

The prior complete exact-mask theorem F1 states: after represented labels
are written as boundary pairs, anchored helpers as boundary triples, and
repeated-bright labels as boundary four-forms, every EE equation using
only represented or repeated-bright labels lies in R10.  This is not a
support-size assertion: a 4+4+4 row could have weight twelve.  F1 excludes
the nonzero quotient rows using the COMPLETE boundary mask census.

F1 and dim(R10)=29 are independently audited in
  push5/even_frontier/EE_QUOTIENT_CHARGE_THEOREM.txt, Sections 1--2;
  push5/root/audit_bright_ee.py;
  push5/root/BRIGHT_EE_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.json.

2. PIVOTS WHEN Q_* IS EMPTY

Perform these ordinary invertible row operations on the full defining-
equation system, in the stated order.

  (a) For every represented even label, choose its BB reference row and
      pivot that label as a boundary pair.

  (b) For every anchored helper, choose one represented-label HB edge and
      pivot that helper as a boundary triple.

  (c) Every missing label is repeated-bright.  Choose an HB edge at an
      anchored helper with r>=2, and pivot that label as a boundary form
      of support at most four.

  (d) Every unanchored helper with u>0 now has an HB row whose even label
      has a four-form.  Choose one such row and pivot the helper as a
      boundary form of support at most five.

Each pivot introduces one previously uneliminated coordinate.  The rows
used in these four stages are distinct: respectively BB, represented HB,
missing HB at an anchored helper, and missing HB at an unanchored helper.
There is no circular substitution or presupposed chronological order of
the expressions.  The operations preserve the complete defining-row span.

Only the h_00 helper coordinates with r=u=0 remain outside B.  They have
no HB rows, by definition, and occur only in HH rows.

Every remaining NON-HH row is a boundary relation in R10:

  * remaining anchored HB rows have support at most 3+4+1=8;
  * remaining unanchored HB rows have support at most 5+4+1=10;
  * every EE row is good and belongs to R10 by the exact-mask fact F1;
  * BB reference substitutions give boundary-only relations of support
    at most four (normalization/Sidonicity usually makes these vacuous).

Every HH row with two anchored endpoints also lies in R10, since its
support is at most 3+3+4=10.  Thus only the z_U remaining HH rows can
supply anything outside R10 or contain an h_00 coordinate.

3. EXACT RESIDUAL-RANK COUNT

Let U denote the h_00 remaining formal helper coordinates.  Their actual
values need not be linearly independent.  They are nonetheless independent
FORMAL coordinates, and each has a kernel relation consisting of that
coordinate plus its expression in the raw-input basis.  Consequently the
projection of the complete residual relation space onto U has rank h_00.

All rows other than the z_U HH rows have zero U projection.  Therefore
the span S of those HH rows projects onto U with rank h_00.  Since
dim(S)<=z_U, its combinations with zero U projection have dimension

                        dim(S intersect B)<=z_U-h_00.

The short rows already lie in R10 and have zero U projection.  Adding
them cannot permit a combination with a nonzero U projection to become
a boundary relation.  The entire boundary relation space is therefore
contained in R10 + (S intersect B), so

                  32 <= 29 + z_U - h_00.

This proves (T).  Sharing of parent signals and cancellation of boundary
coordinates are included in the formal row operations; neither changes
the dimension count.  No helper-degree or canonical-transpose assumption
is used.

The same projection argument, without Q_*=empty and without any pivots,
also proves the universally valid nonnegativities

           z_U-h_00>=0, z-h_00>=0, V0-h_00>=0.             (N)

Indeed r=u=0 helper coordinates occur only in HH rows in the original
system, and the full kernel projects onto all of them.

4. AGGREGATE CUTS

Write p for the number of repeated-bright missing labels, and define

  A = sum_(r=3) u,
  Z = sum (r-1)_+ u,
  E = sum_(r=3) max(u-2,0).

Inherited exact-label capacity and full-helper incompatibility give

                       A<=p,   Z+E<=3p.                   (C)

If Q_* is nonempty, p<=q-1.  If Q_* is empty, (T) makes each of
z-h_00 and V0-h_00 at least three.  Combine these two cases with
the nonnegativities (N) to obtain FOUR valid linear inequalities for
arbitrary z:

              3A - V0 + h_00 <= 3(q-1),                  (L1)
             Z+E - V0 + h_00 <= 3(q-1),                  (L2)
                     3A+h_00 <= 3(q-1)+z,                (L3)
                    Z+E+h_00 <= 3(q-1)+z.                (L4)

No indicator variables or unknown mask bank are needed for these rows.
The coefficient h_00 is exactly the sum of helper-type counts with
r=0 AND u=0.  V0 includes all r=0 types and weights them by v.

For z<=2, (T) rules out Q_*=empty completely.  Thus the stronger
conditional rows are

                   A <= q-1,
                   Z+E <= 3(q-1).                        (L5)

The analogous conclusion holds whenever V0-h_00<=2, if that condition
is known for an integral incidence profile.  Do not apply this conditional
test to a fractional LP point as a continuous implication without a
proper disjunction: the all-z linear rows (L1)--(L4) are always safe.

The inherited z=0,q<=6 rows A<=q-2 and Z+E<=3(q-2) remain stronger
there and should still be retained.  The present theorem extends the
one-exception requirement to z=1,2 and adds the linear incidence budgets
for arbitrary z; it does not replace the stronger special cases.

5. THE TARGET SAVED PROFILE

The first saved transpose profile has

  q=2, z=1, h=16, y=3, x=24,
  1 helper of type (r,u,v)=(0,2,1),
  1 helper of type (1,1,1),
  6 helpers of type (2,1,0),
  8 helpers of type (3,0,0).

It has A=0, Z=6, E=0, h_00=0, V0=1.  Row (L2) requires

                          6-1 <= 3,

which fails by two.  Row (L5) requires 6<=3, failing by three.
This excludes every executable circuit with that aggregate profile.

Its masks also admit a more explicit contradiction.  Z=3q saturates
the per-color capacity and forces both missing colors repeated-bright.
The unique unanchored helper uses both on its two HB edges, so its HH
edge must use a represented label: a fixed even label is a matching at
the odd vertices.  The opposite HH endpoint is the anchored (1,1,1)
helper.  Pivot that endpoint as a triple and this represented HH row
to make the unanchored helper a five-form.  All surviving rows then
lie directly in R10, contradicting the required boundary rank 32.

The generic proof above is stronger: it also excludes the displayed
q=2,z=2 transpose profile and the displayed q=5,z=2 profile without
fixing which HH edge has a represented label.
