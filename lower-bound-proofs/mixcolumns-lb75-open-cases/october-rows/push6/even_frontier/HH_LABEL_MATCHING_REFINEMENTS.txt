EXACT LABEL MATCHING STRENGTHENS THE HH QUOTIENT BUDGET
23 October 2026.  Companion to HH_EXCEPTIONAL_LABEL_BUDGET.txt.

SCOPE

Every normalized acyclic XOR circuit for AES MixColumns transpose is
covered.  Arbitrary intermediate masks, depth, cancellation, parent reuse,
and output reuse remain allowed.  All definitions, the rank-29 space R10,
the full-kernel elimination principle, and finite facts F1/F2/F3/F4 are
as in the prior push5 and current push6 theorems.

These refinements exclude both integral two-phase aggregate points returned
by the first push6 scan.  They do not by themselves exclude the other

1. MATCHING AT THE UNANCHORED HELPER

Split the unanchored helpers into

  U0 = {H:r=u=0},
  U+ = {H:r=0,u>0}.

Write h00=|U0|, V00=sum_U0 v, Vplus=sum_U+ v, and

                    Mplus=sum_U+ min(v,q-u).

A fixed even label is a matching at the odd vertices: given its value e
and one odd endpoint H, the other endpoint is uniquely H+e.  Distinct
defining edges cannot repeat the same unordered three-signal equation
in a normalized acyclic circuit.  Thus the u missing labels on a helper's
HB edges are distinct and cannot appear on any of its HH edges.  Its
number of missing-label HH edges is at most min(v,q-u).

Suppose Q_* is empty.  Use the pivots from the previous theorem:
represented labels have two-forms, anchored helpers three-forms, all
missing labels four-forms, and U+ helpers five-forms.  The only HH rows
that can remain outside R10 or contain a U0 coordinate are:

  (i)  an edge touching U0;
  (ii) an edge joining two U+ helpers;
  (iii) a missing-label edge joining U+ to an anchored helper.

An anchored--anchored HH row has support <=3+3+4=10.  A represented-label
U+--anchored HH row has support <=5+3+2=10, so neither is in this list.

Charge an edge of type (i) one unit to a U0 endpoint.  Charge an edge of
type (ii) one half unit to the v incidence at each U+ endpoint.  Charge
an edge of type (iii) one half to its U+ v incidence and one half to that
endpoint's missing-label HH incidence.  The total charge budget is at most

                         V00 + (Vplus+Mplus)/2.

These charges may overcount some edges, which is safe.  For example, an
edge between U0 and U+ can receive unused half-incidence credit at U+
as well as its full charge at U0.  No potentially long edge is undercounted.

The U0 projection still has full rank h00 and occurs only in rows on this
list.  Eliminating those h00 coordinates consumes at least h00 row
dimensions.  To provide the three required boundary dimensions outside
R10, it follows that

     Q_*=empty ==> D2:=2V00+Vplus+Mplus-2h00 >= 6.           (M)

Universally V00>=h00, from the full-kernel projection onto U0; hence D2>=0.
If Q_* is nonempty, p<=q-1 for the number p of repeated-bright colors.
Using A<=p and Z+E<=3p, exactly the earlier two-case argument proves

                         6A-D2 <= 6(q-1),                 (M1)
                     2(Z+E)-D2 <= 6(q-1).                 (M2)

These are linear helper-type rows for every fixed q.  Their min(v,q-u)
coefficient is known for each type.  They strengthen the previous V0
rows, since D2/2<=V0-h00.  The separately derived z rows can still add
information, and the z<=2 conditional cuts should also be retained.

2. ANCHORED CIRCUITS WITH FULL HELPER--HELPER ENDPOINTS

THEOREM.  Suppose every helper is anchored and every endpoint of every
HH edge has r=3.  If q<=5, then at least TWO missing labels are exceptional.

First, zero exceptions are already excluded: all helpers have three-forms,
all missing labels four-forms, all HH rows have support <=10, and F1 makes
every EE row short.  Boundary rank32 cannot fit in R10 of rank29.

Suppose there is exactly one exceptional label d.  Let qB=q-1<=4 be the
number of other missing labels; all these labels are repeated-bright.
All ordinary HB rows are short after the usual pivots.

CASE A: d IS BRIGHT.

Then d also has a four-form, so all HH rows are short.  An EE row can
escape R10 only by using d and two repeated-bright labels.  Their complete
quotient space has rank at most two by F2.  This cannot provide three
dimensions.  This case is impossible for ANY q and ANY z when all helpers
are anchored; the small-q hypothesis is unnecessary for this case.

CASE B: d IS DARK AND OCCURS ON AN HH EDGE.

Both endpoints are full helpers.  The inherited full-helper incompatibility
allows AT MOST ONE HH edge of a fixed label with both endpoints full.
For missing labels this follows from the complete rank test for two
disjoint full pairs with the same difference; for represented labels a
full/full HH edge is itself impossible.  A shared endpoint is also
excluded by label matching.  See SUPPLEMENT.md Section 1 in the original
75 proof bundle; the property was independently audited in push5.

Pivot d on its unique HH edge.  Its boundary expression is the XOR of
two full-helper triples, so it is a restricted six-form in the sense of
F3 (or a form of support <=4 after cancellation).  All other HH rows are
short because their labels have four-forms.  The only possible new
quotient contributions are EE rows involving d.

Classify such an EE row by its OTHER TWO labels:

  RR: two represented labels.  Their pair forms give d a four-form;
      its difference from the chosen six-form has support <=10.

  RB: one represented and one repeated-bright label.  They give d a
      restricted six-form, because the bright four-form comes from a
      repeated-helper triple plus one boundary coordinate.  All these
      differences together span dimension at most one by F3.

  BB: two repeated-bright labels.  They give d an eight-form and may
      contribute one unrestricted quotient dimension per row.

For a fixed actual d, the pairs of other labels in DISTINCT EE equations
are disjoint: d=a+b and d=a+c force b=c, making the equations identical.
Thus BB rows consume disjoint pairs of the qB bright labels.

There are at most two BB rows.  If there are two, they use all four bright
labels, so no RB row can exist.  The RR rows contribute zero, and the
total quotient rank is at most two.  If there is at most one BB row,
its contribution plus the entire RB difference space has rank at most
1+1=2.  Again three dimensions are impossible.

CASE C: d IS DARK AND OCCURS ON NO HH EDGE.

It has no HB edge either, since every helper is anchored and it is dark;
it has no BB reference by definition.  Its coordinate occurs only in EE
rows.  There must be at least one such row, by the full-kernel property.

If an RR row exists, pivot d on it as a four-form.  Other RR and RB rows
then have support <=10.  The disjoint BB pairs give at most two possibly
long rows, so quotient rank<=2.

Otherwise, if an RB row exists, pivot d on it as a restricted six-form.
This pivot consumes one bright label in the fixed-d pair matching.
The remaining RB rows together give at most one quotient dimension by
F3, and at most one BB row is possible among the remaining <=3 bright
labels.  Quotient rank is at most two.

Otherwise every d row is BB.  There are at most two such rows total;
pivoting d consumes one, leaving at most one boundary relation.

Every possible case contradicts the required quotient dimension three.
This proves the theorem.

3. LINEARIZATION OF THE SMALL-q THEOREM

Let h0 count all unanchored helpers, and let

                         W=sum_(r=1 or r=2) v.

Assume q<=5.  If h0=W=0, the theorem gives p<=q-2.  If h0=0 and W>=1,
the previously proved all-anchored obstruction still gives p<=q-1.
If h0>=1, the unconditional p<=q suffices.  Because these are integer
incidence counts in an actual circuit, the three cases imply

                         p <= q-2+2h0+W.

Consequently, for EVERY transpose circuit with q<=5,

                     A-2h0-W <= q-2,                     (S1)
                 Z+E-6h0-3W <= 3(q-2).                   (S2)

These are valid linear inequalities on the convex relaxation as well:
the proof covers every actual integral incidence profile, and the final
inequalities themselves are linear.  No conditional rule is being
applied to fractional helper counts during optimization.

5. ADDITIONAL ALL-BRIGHT COROLLARY

If every helper is anchored and EVERY missing label is bright, then

                             A <= q-3

for arbitrary z.  Indeed all missing labels have four-forms and all HH
rows have support <=10.  If at most two missing labels fail to be
full-bright, the entire EE quotient space has rank at most two by F4,
contradicting the required three dimensions.  Full-helper missing
contacts are distinct colors, so A is the full-bright color count.

This is explicitly conditional on all missing labels being bright.
It is NOT inserted into the aggregate scalar model unless that condition
is separately established; neither q nor the helper-type counts alone
automatically establish it.
