ROUNDED CLASS-CORE INEQUALITIES FROM EXACT OUTPUT MASKS
2026-10-23

THEOREM

Let a normalized binary XOR circuit compute AES MixColumns M or M^T.
For an odd helper, r counts its ACTUAL represented H--B incidences and
u its ACTUAL missing-label H--B incidences.  Let q be the number of
missing even labels.  Put t=5 for M and t=7 for M^T.

For every positive integer d, the following is necessary:

  sum_(r>=2) floor((r-1+u)/d)*n_(r,u,v)
     <= min_(1<=k<=t) floor[ t*floor((4*k+q)/d) / k ].       (1)

Only d<=q+2 can have nonzero coefficients, because r<=3 and u<=q.
The d=1 case is the previous global repeated-contact core inequality.
The final scalar model appends d=2,...,q+2, taking the strongest
right-hand side over k for each divisor.

The theorem applies to arbitrary depth, cancellation, output reuse,
HH edges, and EE gates.  It does not prescribe values for helpers
having r<2.  The final row is linear in the actual helper-type counts;
their integrality is used in its mathematical derivation, not silently
assumed of a numerical LP witness.

1. THE EXACT MASK FACT THAT LOCALIZES THE CORE

The complete boundary-triple census gives all possible repeated helper
values (r>=2): 440 for M and 616 for M^T.  Every expression of a given
candidate uses output coordinates from exactly ONE bit-position class.
Each class has exactly four output coordinates.  There are t classes.

The strengthened contact-pair fact is:

  If distinct repeated candidates H,K satisfy H+K=B_i+B_j, every
  output among B_i,B_j belongs to the class of H or the class of K.

The raw input coordinates are unrestricted in this statement.  It is
checked for all 11,520 forward and 16,992 transpose candidate pairs
with a boundary-pair difference.  Pairs not realizable together are
included, so the census is a superset of actual possibilities.

2. OWNERSHIP FOR EVERY SUBSET OF CLASSES

Choose any k of the t classes, and take all actual repeated helpers in
those classes.  Select their represented H--B gates and the required
distinct B--B reference gates.  For each missing label with at least
two contacts among these selected helpers, select those contacts too.

The strengthened pair fact puts every output anchor of the additional
contacts in the same chosen k classes.  The actual-birth ownership
argument of REPEATED_CONTACT_CORE_PROOF.txt therefore works with
C replaced by 4k.  It gives

  E_rep(S) + sum_e max(0,m_e(S)-1) <= 4k,

and, since there are at most q colors,

  E_rep(S) + U_rep(S) <= 4k+q.                             (2)

Define the integer helper weight w_H=(r_H-1)+u_H for r_H>=2.
Equation (2) is precisely sum_(H in S) w_H<=4k+q.

3. FLOOR LOCALLY, THEN COUNT HOW OFTEN EACH HELPER OCCURS

For a fixed divisor d, the sum of the individual floors is no larger
than the floor of the sum.  Thus every k-class subset obeys

  sum_(H in S) floor(w_H/d) <= floor((4k+q)/d).             (3)

Sum (3) over ALL binom(t,k) class subsets of size k.  Each helper
belongs to exactly binom(t-1,k-1) of them.  Consequently, writing
T_d for the total sum of floor(w_H/d) over repeated helpers,

  binom(t-1,k-1)*T_d <= binom(t,k)*floor((4k+q)/d),

so

  T_d <= (t/k)*floor((4k+q)/d).

T_d is an integer in every actual circuit.  Taking the final floor
and minimizing over k proves (1).

This last integer step yields a valid linear inequality for all
circuits.  A continuous relaxation can include that proved inequality
without claiming that its own variable values are integers.

4. A CONCRETE EXCLUSION: NINE REPEATED HELPERS AT q=7

The two selected integral M witnesses after the first push6 scan had
six r=2,u=2 helpers and three r=3,u=1 helpers, with q=7.  Every one
has w=3.  Take t=5,d=3,k=4 in (1):

  T_3 <= floor[(5/4)*floor((16+7)/3)]
      = floor[(5/4)*7] = 8.

Those vectors instead have T_3=9.  Therefore neither can represent
an actual AES circuit, irrespective of its HH degree realization.

Equivalently, placing nine such helpers in five classes leaves some
class with at most one.  The other four classes then contain at least
eight helpers, requiring weight at least 24.  Equation (2) permits
only 16+7=23.  This simple contradiction captures the additional
information supplied by the exact output classes.

The earlier saturated_M_class_assignments.py independently enumerated
1,271 missing-contact graphs, reduced them to 21 permutation orbits,
and found no assignment after 3,875 backtracking nodes.  The analytic
argument above supersedes that enumeration for the exclusion.  It
requires neither the class adjacency graph nor the extra missing-pair
restrictions used by that more detailed check.
