WHAT THE EXACT MASKS ADDED
-------------------------
All helpers with at least two ACTUAL represented H--B incidences belong
to a complete boundary-triple census: 440 candidate values for M and 616
for M transpose.  Every such candidate belongs to one output-bit class;
there are 5 classes for M and 7 for M transpose, with 4 outputs per class.

If two such helper values differ by a boundary pair, every output
endpoint of that pair lies in one of those two helper classes.  The
complete census checks 11,520 forward and 16,992 transpose contact pairs,
with no exception.  This was independently rebuilt from AES arithmetic
by the root audit and by this branch.  Arbitrary other helpers retain
unrestricted masks.

For any selected class subset S, actual-birth ownership now gives

  E_rep(S)+sum_e max(0,m_e(S)-1) <= 4*|S|,
  E_rep(S)+U_rep(S) <= 4*|S|+p.

Here E_rep=N2+2*N3, U_rep counts missing-label contacts at repeated
helpers, m_e counts those contacts for one color, and p is the number
of missing colors with any repeated contact.  Always p<=q.  A color
shared across multiple repeated helpers cannot be reused without
consuming the remaining birth capacity in their exact output core.

The full-class selection and the inherited per-color capacity 3 yield

  N2+2*N3+U_rep <= C+p,
  2*N2+10*N3+3*U_rep <= 2*x+2*C+3*p,
  N2+5*N3+2*U_rep+A <= x+C+3*p,

with C=20/28.  A counts full-helper missing incidences.  The first
push5 selected M witness violates the first row by 4: 28<=24 would be
required.  Its exact shared endpoints leave no room for those births.

The localized theorem also yields rounded class inequalities.  Put
w_H=r_H-1+u_H for repeated helpers.  For t=C/4 classes, every d>=1 gives

  sum_H floor(w_H/d)
    <= min_(1<=k<=t) floor[(t/k)*floor((4*k+p)/d)].

The implemented row uses the proved CONSTANT upper bound q_eff for p
inside these floors.  For M,q=7,d=3,k=4 the bound is 8.  A returned
vector with nine repeated helpers all having w=3 therefore fails 9<=8.
This exact class obstruction supersedes a larger contact-graph search.

A second family follows from sum_c max(W_c-4,0)<=p, where W_c is the
total weight in a class.  For rational 0<=a/d<=1,

  sum_H floor(a*w_H/d) <= t*floor(4*a/d)+p.

All nontrivial distinct rows of this one-parameter family are covered
by reduced positive fractions with denominator<=max(4,q+2).  The
omitted initial row 0<=p is true by definition.
