INDEPENDENT LOGICAL AUDIT OF THE PUSH6 CORE AND EXCEPTIONAL-LABEL CUTS
23 October 2026. Reviewer: odd_spine_frontier. Push5 files are unchanged.

This note audits the deductions independently of their authors. Finite
exact-mask facts are explicitly identified as inputs and are independently
regenerated by the root audit files. No numerical optimizer result is used
as a premise in the arguments below.

1. REPEATED-HELPER CORE OWNERSHIP: PASS

Definitions: N2,N3 count actual helpers with respectively two and three
represented-label HB defining edges. Erep=N2+2N3. Urep is the sum of their
missing-label HB degrees. q is the number of missing labels. C=20 for M
and C=28 for Mt.

Finite inputs, checked by push6/root/audit_exact_mask_core.py:

  * Every actual r>=2 helper is among the complete 440/616 candidates.
  * All of their represented triple forms use only the C core outputs.
  * Whenever two such candidate values differ by a boundary pair, both
    endpoints are among the raw inputs and the same C core outputs.

For a missing label e touching m>=2 selected helpers, write its contacts
as Hi+Bi=e. For every i, choose a different j; Hi+Hj=Bi+Bj. Candidate
values are distinct, so Bi and Bj are distinct. The finite pair fact puts
every boundary anchor Bi in the core. This is the only new mask step.

Select all R=2N2+3N3 represented HB rows at these helpers, their L distinct
actual BB reference rows, and all m HB rows for each multirepeated missing
color. These are distinct actual gate equations. Their possible noninput
birth values are contained in:

  N2+N3 helpers, L represented labels, C core outputs,
  and one new missing label per multirepeated color.

Every selected gate has a distinct birth result in that bank. Therefore

              Erep + sum_{m>=2}(m-1) <= C.

Since Urep-q=sum_all_colors(m-1), and zero-contact colors contribute -1,

                    Erep+Urep <= C+q.

Sharing, cancellation, HH equations and EE equations do not alter the
selected birth injection. No assumptions about how the candidate helper
values were computed are made.

The stronger OO charge also passes. An EE-born label in the selected bank
must be born outside these selected odd-edge equations. There are at least
L+q_multi-x such labels, and at most C-Erep-U_multi+q_multi outside birth
slots. Hence

                    L+Erep+U_multi <= x+C.

2. LOCALIZED CLASS OWNERSHIP: PASS

Reviewed push6/root/LOCALIZED_SHARING_THEOREM.txt. Additional finite inputs:
each repeated candidate has one output-bit class; every boundary output
in a candidate-pair difference belongs to the two candidates' classes;
multicontact colors meet at most two adjacent classes in the reported graph.

For any class subset S, select only actual repeated helpers in S. The same
two-contact argument places all selected multicolor anchors within the
4|S| output bank. Thus, with m_e(S) the selected contact count,

    Erep(S)+sum_e max(0,m_e(S)-1) <= 4|S|,
    L(S)+Erep(S)+U_multi(S) <= x+4|S|.

The latter uses the global x and cannot be summed over subsets without
another allocation argument. A collection of saturated classes, each with
Erep({k})=4, has no color touching two repeated helpers in the collection.
The exact adjacency restriction is independent of this counting argument.

3. NO EXCEPTIONAL LABELS FORCE THREE HH DIMENSIONS: PASS

Reviewed push6/even_frontier/HH_EXCEPTIONAL_LABEL_BUDGET.txt. This theorem is
for Mt. Its finite inputs are dim R10=29, full boundary kernel dimension32,
and F1: every EE row using only represented or repeated-bright labels has
zero quotient modulo R10 after the stated boundary pivots.

Assume Q*=empty, meaning every missing label touches an actual r>=2 helper.
The pivots represented label->pair, anchored helper->triple, missing
label->four-form, unanchored helper with u>0->five-form are all legitimate
distinct-coordinate row eliminations. They need not respect circuit time:
they operate on its exact independent defining-equation system.

Only h00 formal helper coordinates with r=u=0 remain. Every non-HH row
lies in R10. Anchored--anchored HH rows also have support at most3+3+4=10.
Only zU HH rows touching an unanchored helper can contribute nonshort rows
or h00 coordinates. The full residual relation space projects onto those
h00 formal coordinates with rank h00: each coordinate has its own relation
to the raw-input basis, irrespective of dependence among its actual masks.

If S is the span of the zU rows, then
dim(S intersect boundary)=dim(S)-h00<=zU-h00. Adding short rows cannot
cancel a nonzero helper projection. Filling the missing three boundary
dimensions therefore requires

                        zU-h00 >= 3.

This is sound with arbitrary EE orientations, cancellation, sharing, HH
connectivity and output reuse. In general zU-h00>=0; also V0-h00>=0 with
V0=sum_(r=0)v, because zU<=V0. No assumption that all h00 masks are
linearly independent is made or needed.

4. RESTRICTING THE HIGH-EXCESS BUDGET TO REPEATED-BRIGHT COLORS: PASS

This small step is needed for the new aggregate inequalities and is not
just an unproved per-color interpretation of E. Let P be the ACTUAL
repeated-bright missing colors, p=|P|. Define

  A=sum_(r=3)u, Z=sum_(r>=2)(r-1)u,
  E=sum_(r=3)max(u-2,0).

The full-helper incompatibility theorem gives A<=p. The proof in
research_deep/helpers/HIGH_EXCESS_CAPACITY.md restricts to P as follows.
Let Q be its complete high-excess palette and pL=|P\Q|. Local weighted
capacity gives Z<=3p-pL. Every actual full helper touches at most two Q
colors, so its max(u-2,0) overflow can be charged to incidences at P\Q.
At most one actual full helper touches each missing color, hence E<=pL.
Consequently

                         Z+E <= 3p.

Exceptional colors touch no r>=2 helper, so deleting them changes none of
A,Z,E, or any full helper's u. This is the reason the restriction works.

Case splitting Q* nonempty (p<=q-1) against Q* empty (zU-h00>=3) gives

    3A-(V0-h00) <= 3(q-1),  Z+E-(V0-h00) <= 3(q-1),
    3A-(z-h00)  <= 3(q-1),  Z+E-(z-h00)  <= 3(q-1).

For z<=2, Q* must be nonempty, giving A<=q-1 and Z+E<=3(q-1).
These conclusions introduce no new rank-independence assumptions.

5. REFINED UNANCHORED-ENDPOINT HH BUDGET: PASS

Let U0 be helpers with r=u=0, Uplus helpers with r=0,u>0. Put

  V00=sum_U0 v, Vplus=sum_Uplus v,
  Mplus=sum_Uplus min(v,q-u).

Under Q*=empty, potentially nonshort HH rows are only:
U0--anything, Uplus--Uplus, or Uplus--anchored in a missing color.
Charge one to a U0 endpoint; charge half a v-unit to each Uplus endpoint;
and charge another half to each missing-color Uplus incidence. This pays
every potentially long row, possibly overpaying some. Every helper has
at most one odd-edge incidence per actual even label, so its missing HH
degree is at most min(v,q-u). Hence the number of potentially long rows
is at most V00+(Vplus+Mplus)/2. The h00 projection consumes h00 dimensions:

             B=2V00+Vplus+Mplus-2h00 >= 6.

Unconditionally B>=0, since V00>=h00. The same case split therefore gives

                2(Z+E)-B <= 6(q-1),
                      6A-B <= 6(q-1).

The min coefficient depends on q. It is a linear type coefficient when
q is fixed in an enumerated integer profile, not a new unrestricted LP
linear expression with q left variable.

6. ALL ANCHORED, HH FULL/FULL, q<=5: TWO EXCEPTIONS REQUIRED

This additional conditional refinement passes given the inherited finite
F2 and F3 ranks. Assume all helpers are anchored and every HH edge has full
helper endpoints. Zero exceptions is impossible because then every row
lies in R10. One weak-bright exception gives quotient rank at most two by
the complete fixed-exception 4+4+4 audit, while all non-EE rows stay short.

For one dark label d, the other qB=q-1<=4 missing labels are repeated-
bright. SUPPLEMENT.md Section1 permits at most one d-colored full/full HH
edge. If present, pivot d by that row into a restricted six-form. All RR
EE rows are short; all RB differences span at most one quotient dimension
by F3. BB rows use disjoint bright-label pairs because e+f=d uniquely
forces each partner. If two BB rows exist they exhaust all four bright
labels, leaving no RB row. If at most one exists, RB+BB capacity is at
most1+1. Thus at most two of the three necessary dimensions are supplied.

If there is no d-HH row, d occurs only in EE rows. A direct low-q audit
avoids relying on the longer earlier q>=7 argument:

  * If any RR row exists, pivot it. d becomes a four-form, all RB rows
    become short, and at most two BB rows can remain.
  * Otherwise, if any RB row exists, pivot it. d is a restricted six-
    form. RB differences have rank at most one. The pivot consumes one
    bright label's unique partner, leaving at most one BB pair.
  * Otherwise only BB rows exist, at most two total. After one pivot,
    at most one boundary row remains.

Every case has quotient capacity below three. Thus these conditional
profiles require at least two exceptional colors. As before the capacity
proof restricted to P then yields A<=q-2 and Z+E<=3(q-2).

7. ROUNDED CLASS-SUBSET OWNERSHIP: PASS

Let t=5 for M or t=7 for Mt. Each actual repeated helper has one of these
classes. For its integer weight w_H=r_H-1+u_H, localized ownership implies
that every k-class subset S satisfies

                     sum_{H in S} w_H <= 4k+q.

For each positive integer d, the sum of floor(w_H/d) over that subset is
at most floor((4k+q)/d). Summing over all binom(t,k) subsets counts every
helper exactly binom(t-1,k-1) times. The full helper count is an integer,
so the valid aggregate inequality is

   sum_H floor(w_H/d) <= floor[(t/k)*floor((4k+q)/d)].

It holds for every 1<=k<=t and d>=1. A minimum over k can be used.
For M,q=7,d=3,k=4 the right side is floor(5*7/4)=8. The two selected
push6 M vectors each have six (r,u)=(2,2) and three (3,1) helpers, hence
nine helpers of weight3. Their left side is9, proving infeasibility.
Neither color-graph enumeration nor exact adjacency is required.

8. ADDITIONAL OVERLOAD AND RATIONAL-ROUNDING CONSEQUENCES

Let W_c=sum_{H in class c} w_H. Apply the localized sum inequality to
the subset of overloaded classes {c:W_c>4}. It gives

                    sum_c max(W_c-4,0) <= q.              (O)

Thus these repeated-helper weights must fit in t classes with a common
four-unit baseline and at most q total excess units. In addition every
class separately has sum(r_H-1)<=4, since it has only four represented-
relation dimensions. Exact class/mask compatibility can be stricter.

For integers 0<=a<=d, every nonnegative integer W satisfies

 floor(aW/d) <= floor(4a/d)+max(W-4,0).

For W<=4 this is monotonicity. For W=4+j, j>=0 integer, it follows from
floor(4a/d+aj/d)<=floor(4a/d)+j because aj/d<=j. Applying this to every
class, and using sum_H floor(a*w_H/d)<=floor(a*W_c/d) within a class,
proves another valid family:

       sum_H floor(a*w_H/d) <= t*floor(4a/d)+q.            (R)

At a/d=2/3, a population of N helpers all having w=3 must obey
2N<=2t+q. At t=5,q=7, this again rules out N=9. Unlike the preceding
unit-numerator subset family, (R) can assign larger rounded coefficients
to other weight values. It is a valid necessary inequality on the circuit
convex hull, not a proof that every passing type vector is assignable.

9. GENERIC COUPLING THROUGH THE ACTUAL REPEATED-BRIGHT COUNT: PASS

Reviewed push6/global_chronology/P_COUPLED_RESOURCE_ROWS.txt. Let p count
ACTUAL missing labels having at least one HB contact at an r>=2 helper.
These are exactly the repeated-bright labels already used in Sections3--6.
All colors with m=0 are absent from these ownership counts. Therefore the
core and OO resources hold with p in place of q:

    Erep+Urep <= C+p,
    2N2+10N3+3Urep <= 2x+2C+3p,
    N2+5N3+2Urep+A <= x+C+3p.

For completeness, from L+Erep+U_multi<=x+C and L>=3N3 we have
N2+5N3+U_multi<=x+C. For a contacted color let m be its repeated-contact
count and f its full-contact count. The inherited capacities are
f<=1 and m+f<=3. Write g=m if m>=2, else0. Directly for the possible
integer pairs (m,f),

                  2g>=3(m-1),   g>=2m+f-3.

Summing over the p contacted colors and substituting gives the two OO
rows above. No zero-contact color is needed to pay either inequality.
Likewise every localized class subset uses at most p colors; the overload
budget in Section8 strengthens to sum_c max(W_c-4,0)<=p, and its rational
floor resources have right side t*floor(4a/d)+p.

For Mt the audited exceptional-color arguments give all the following
budgets for this SAME p:

    p <= q-1+h0,
    3p <= 3(q-1)+V0-h00,
    3p <= 3(q-1)+z-h00,
    6p <= 6(q-1)+D2.

The first follows from the all-anchored obstruction when h0=0 and p<=q
when h0>=1. The remaining three are the Q* empty/nonempty case splits
already audited, now applied directly to p. Their defect terms are
unconditionally nonnegative. The F5 extension in Section10 also gives
p<=q-2+2h0+W when q<=6 OR y<=3, where W=sum_(r=1,2)v.
The constant budgets p<=q-1 for z<=2 and p<=q-2 for z=0,q<=6 remain valid.

The generic generation rule is exact: for a resource K(n)<=base+c*p and
a budget s*p-g(n)<=b, with c>=0 and s>0,

                  s*K(n)-c*g(n) <= s*base+c*b.

Multiply the resource by s and use cs*p<=c*(b+g(n)). All quantities and
the sign requirements have the correct direction. This applies equally
to ownership, A<=p, Z+E<=3p, and the rational overload-floor family.

The subset-averaged floor bound may replace q only by a proved CONSTANT
upper bound on p. A variable helper-count budget is not silently inserted
inside its floors. The final generic linear coupling avoids that error.
Deduplicating identical coefficient vectors by the smallest right side
preserves validity. Dividing a row by a common divisor of both its integer
coefficients and its right side is exact even for fractional LP variables.

10. FULL/FULL HH ZERO-QUOTIENT EXTENSION F5: PASS

Reviewed push6/even_frontier/FULL_PAIR_HH_ZERO_QUOTIENT.txt and the root's
independent PASS report push6/root/FULL_PAIR_DARK_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.json.
This logical audit uses that finite report as a premise; it does not
claim a third independent regeneration of its million-row census.

The report has 112 full candidates, 616 repeated candidates, 2,016 pair
values R, and 30,340 repeated four-form values P outside R. For the 4,608
possible full-pair differences d outside P union R, it checks all 4,800
individual full-pair pivots. Their 917,376 P/P and 199,184 R/P EE
alternatives all reduce to zero as full 64-coordinate rows modulo the
rank-29 short-relation basis. Since the previously audited full R10 rank
is29, that basis is R10. The remaining R/R alternatives have support
at most6+2+2=10 and are short analytically.

Actual dark incidence does NOT imply d is outside P union R. Those cases
are now covered explicitly and correctly:

  * If d has a conceivable pair form, it differs from the actual full/full
    HH six-form by a true relation of support<=8. Replace it modulo R10;
    an EE row then has support<=2+4+4=10.
  * If d has a conceivable repeated four-form, the difference has support
    <=10. Replace it modulo R10. Rows with a pair-form partner have
    support<=10, while three repeated four-forms are covered by F1.

Neither replacement asserts an actual BB or HB incidence for d. These
are valid quotient substitutions in boundary forms, so arbitrary sharing
and cancellation cause no gap. The root independently checks all 504 R
and 912 P full-candidate-pair replacement cases as well. Alternative
triple/four-form choices differ by short true relations, hence one form
per value suffices.

Consequently, when every helper is anchored and every HH endpoint is
full, a lone dark exceptional label cannot occur on an HH row. There is
at most one such full/full row at a fixed color; use it as the pivot.
All remaining HH rows are short, all EE rows involving d are short by
F5, and all other EE rows are short by F1. This cannot fill the three
missing quotient dimensions. A lone weak-bright exception is already
impossible by F2. Thus a sole exception must be dark and EE-only.

Its formal coordinate must be eliminated by one EE pivot. At least three
further EE rows are necessary for three quotient dimensions, so y>=4.
The q>=7 argument also passes with the following complete RB case split:

  * RR pivot: d has a four-form, all RB/RR alternatives are short; at
    least three BB rows use six distinct bright labels.
  * RB pivot: d is a restricted six-form. All RB differences together
    have rank<=1. If at least three BB rows occur, their six bright
    labels plus the pivot's label already suffice to force qB>=7.
    Otherwise exactly two BB rows and a nonzero additional RB difference
    are needed: the pivot, two BB pairs, and that RB row use1+4+1=6
    distinct bright labels. With fewer than two BB rows rank3 is impossible.
  * Only BB pivots: four rows are needed, using eight bright labels.

The matching uses the forced actual partner equation d=a+b, so another
EE row cannot reuse one of those partner labels. Therefore qB=q-1>=6,
and a sole exception requires q>=7 as well as y>=4.

This extends the Section6 two-exception theorem to q<=6 OR y<=3.
The integer incidence case split yields, for every Mt circuit in that
scalar scope,

    p<=q-2+2h0+W,
    A-2h0-W<=q-2,
    Z+E-6h0-3W<=3(q-2).

If h0=W=0 there are at least two exceptions; if h0=0,W>=1 there is
at least one; if h0>=1 the bound p<=q suffices. These are linear valid
inequalities, so including them in a fractional relaxation is legitimate.
F5 makes no claim about EE rows using two or three exceptional labels.

11. AUDIT LIMIT

This note certifies the logical implications of the named finite facts.
It does not claim an exhaustive search over full XOR circuits, a new
global numerical lower bound, or simultaneous realizability of aggregate
helper-degree profiles. Those would require separate evidence.
