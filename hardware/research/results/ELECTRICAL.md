# Electrical checks: max slew, max capacitance, max fanout

Generated by `python3 hardware/research/electrical.py` from OpenSTA's `report_check_types -max_slew -max_capacitance -max_fanout -violators`, which hwbench/flow.py writes after synthesis, placement and routing of every job. These are electrical design-rule checks of STA against the Liberty limits (pin transition, net load, fanout). They are **not routing DRC**: detailed routing's design-rule count is 0 for every reported job (0 of 14243 routed jobs non-zero; route_drc.rpt empty in all but 0). The flow runs no buffering, sizing or repair, so a violation is what synthesis, or the circuit itself in the preserve flow, produced. Jobs are the study's manifest (manifest.py: the benchmark's jobs and every research variant's), every one of them counted below, including those without any violation. [electrical.csv](electrical.csv) lists **only the job-stage reports with at least one violation** (425 rows); all counts and distributions: [electrical.json](electrical.json).
