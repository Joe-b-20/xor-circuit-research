# NIST's AES MixColumns and InvMixColumns circuits

Byte-identical copies of the NIST circuits that the hardware benchmark compares with XORFlux's
AES MixColumns and InvMixColumns circuits, from [NIST's circuit repository](https://github.com/usnistgov/Circuits)
at commit `3d807741` (the commit of the S-box seeds in `search/nonlinear/seeds/`).
[MANIFEST.json](MANIFEST.json) gives each file's path and URL at that commit and its SHA-256.
NIST's S-box circuits are those of [search/nonlinear/seeds/](../../search/nonlinear/seeds/).
