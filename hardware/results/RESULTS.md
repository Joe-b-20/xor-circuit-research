All rows per job: [results.csv](results.csv).

Area: standard-cell area of the logic cells (no taps, no input-port buffers). Delay: worst input-to-output path through a buffer after each input port, inputs driven by the library's X1 buffer, 3.898 fF (Nangate45) or 5 fF (SKY130HD) on each output; *synth* without wires, *routed* with OpenRCX parasitics. ADP = area x routed delay. Power: OpenSTA after routing, at 100 MHz with a new random input every cycle and zero-delay switching activity (glitches not counted), exact for the S-boxes (all inputs) and estimated from the simulated inputs for the 32-bit linear maps.
