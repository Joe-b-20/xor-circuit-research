## G/T5 — lower_bound: the proof side — `W6/lower_bound/` (1 core)

**Idea.** Settle 87 from the other direction. A structure theorem makes the depth-3

**Scale.** Model: **7,897 candidate level-2 masks, 496 candidate edges, 6,120
decompositions** (`W6/lower_bound/THEOREM.md`). Certificate: **23,450 nonzero rational
duals**. Controls: `control_cuts.py` over 23,405 inequalities and `control_lp.py` over
72,830 rows, both PASS at objective exactly 65 = 97 − 32. `lp_level.py 4`: 92,143 rows,
**47.59824561403512** vs level 1's **47.59824561403509**.

**Outcome: THEOREM — unconditional within its scope, and the strongest proof-side
result the project holds.**

> **Every 2-input-XOR circuit of depth ≤ 3 computing the 32×32 GF(2) AES MixColumns
> matrix has at least 80 gates. Bracket: 80 ≤ N_depth3 ≤ 97.**

It is depth-conditional in *scope* (depth ≤ 3 only) but unconditional *within* that
scope: no vocabulary restriction, no solver trust, no floating point. Re-checkable
offline in one command (`python3 certify.py --check cert_depth3.json`). Certified
by-products: every depth-3 circuit has ≥ 18 level-1 edges, plus a per-E certified LP
profile for E = 18…31, and the C7 argument (degree histogram 1:5020 2:2052 3:584
4:157 5:68 6:16, max degree 6 ⇒ |L2| ≥ ⌈44/6⌉ = 8) closing E ≥ 49.
