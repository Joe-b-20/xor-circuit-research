# Review of the multiwindow counterexample family

Independent hand review of `continuation/MULTIWINDOW.md`. No SAT, enumeration, exact-gate search, or computer-driven case elimination was used. The claimed exact event-free optimum is established by a symbolic lower bound and matching construction, not a gate search.

The family and its stated asymptotic conclusion are correct. In the displayed circuit, each prefix `p_ik` has an event-free path to `A_ik`, and every `A_ik` is an output. The common value `a=x+y` is nonlocal and all paths from it pass through the output events `T_i`. Hence it is the sole hidden gate, there are no hidden events, and the nonlocal-operand set for every selected event is the same singleton `{a}`. Every gate value is distinct and non-input; the circuit is reduced and live. Each selected target `T_i` has its own nonzero `Z_i` coordinate, so the targets are independent. The stated window-size, block-dimension, maximal-clique and no-reciprocal-own-block properties hold.

The event-free lower bound is valid. In a live event-free circuit every gate containing a private block `W_ik` feeds its sole corresponding output, and so cannot also contain any other private block. After bypassing duplicates, the first combination involving `w_ik` and a different value is distinct for each `(i,k)`. Zeroing all private blocks makes each such gate a wire and preserves event-freeness; at least `rm` gates can be removed. In the projected output problem, the `rm` distinct values `P_ik` require distinct gates carrying `Y`. Each `T_i` has an event-free ancestor cone supported on `{X,Z_i}` that avoids `Y` and needs at least `m` binary gates to combine its `m+1` essential input coordinates. The cones for different `i` overlap only on the singleton input block `X`, so after removing duplicate values they have no shared physical gates. This gives a further `2rm` lower bound. The stated clean-prefix construction meets it and adding `rm` private-input gates gives an event-free construction of `3rm`. Thus `L_eventfree=3rm` exactly.

The constructed circuit has `2rm+r+1` gates, so the signed difference `L_eventfree-G` is `r(m-1)-1`. It is strictly positive when `r(m-1)>1` and tends to infinity with `r` for fixed `m>=2`. At `r=1,m=2` the constructed circuit ties the event-free minimum, so a claim that it lies strictly below the minimum for every allowed parameter value would be inaccurate; the author was alerted. For the highlighted fixed `m=3` the difference is `2r-1>0` for every `r>=1`.

No conclusion about optimality of the cancellation-using circuit or a new MixColumns bound follows. The family does show that the listed general geometric and independence properties, even with bounded block sizes, do not make distinct-window hidden credits additive or bound one hidden gate's number of local partners.

The author corrected the boundary wording. I also checked the affine-completion calculation now in the direction note: every vector of `a+K` contains `y`, locality excludes `x`, and every output window containing `Y` includes at most one `Z_i`. Thus the local completions are exactly `y+S_i=p_im`; their differences `S_i+S_1` are independent for `i=2,...,r`, lie in `K`, and span a rank-`r-1` space. The resulting affine-quotient rank is one and is attained by the sole hidden operand `a`. This is a symbolic argument over all subsets, not enumeration.
