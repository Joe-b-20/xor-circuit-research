Neither description is presented as a classification of the one-tail case.

The fixed seven-gate circuit is correctly used only to refute an unrestricted extension of even-wire separation. It is not described as a smaller mixed-label circuit or as an optimal construction. The proposed next direction is clearly a research judgment rather than a proved charging theorem.
