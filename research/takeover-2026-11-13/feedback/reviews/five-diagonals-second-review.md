# Second independent review: five diagonals

Hand review of `../FIVE_DIAGONALS.md`. No SAT, exhaustive enumeration, circuit search, exact-gate search, or computer-driven case elimination was used. This is not formal verification.

The thirteen-gate five-diagonal lower bound and its unrestricted ten-consumer consequence check out under the stated hypotheses. Four own triple masks and five distinct nonzero even diagonal masks require nine non-input own first occurrences. Three entries are necessary to span the row free parts and cannot be first occurrences, so an at-most-twelve-gate circuit has exactly nine first occurrences and three entries. Those three labels form an even-space basis, so every free part is even.

The five clean values span the three-dimensional diagonal `D`. For the span `V` of physical even values, the kernel of `rho` is therefore exactly `D`. The formal odd-pair relation bound yields `9<=13-c-r`; the eight distinct named odd residues give `c>=2^(3-r)`, forcing `r>=2`. Thus at least two even values are nonclean. Four odd row gates then leave at most one odd helper.

With no odd helper, at most three nonclean even gates have named-pair representations and the even-only formal-relation nullity is at most three, for rank at most six. With one helper, there are exactly five clean and two nonclean even gates; their odd-pair relations number at most one plus two per nonclean value, and the even-only nullity is at most two. The resulting rank is at most seven. Both contradict the nine independent physical non-entry equations. The argument permits cancellation and does not depend on a finite circuit search.

For a ten-gate consumer, the actual entry labels lie in and span the rank-three clean diagonal. If total joint cost were at most 21, rank-three source replay would use at most twelve source gates. A cost-preserving replay would contradict the augmented twelve-gate minimum, so the one possible extra-gate step must occur. Its three available independent diagonal values, the newly materialized pair sum, and the returned triple sum are five distinct nonzero diagonal values. The completed replay would supply them and the four source rows in at most twelve gates, contradicting the five-diagonal lower bound. Hence every joint circuit with exactly ten consumer-bearing gates has at least 22 gates, allowing feedback. This does not exclude a smaller joint circuit with eleven or more consumer-bearing gates.
