# Review of the online repair lower bound

2026-11-13. Independent machine proof review of [ONLINE_REPAIR](../ONLINE_REPAIR.md). No circuit search or finite-family checks were run.

With \(m\) distinct nonzero available values, at most \(m+\binom m2\) nonzero values are available or obtainable by one XOR. If \(m<M(d)\), some nonzero value \(t\) is outside that set. The adversary can build \(z+t\) from \(z\) and the coordinate units in \(t\), then exit by adding \(z\). The intermediate gates are consumer-bearing and give no free source-only values. At the instant of selection, \(t\) requires at least two new source-only gates. The strategy may learn its mask while the chain is revealed, but both gates are still charged to the interval ending at that exit. Intervals for successive requests are disjoint.

If proactive work reaches \(M(d)\) before another request, it is already charged. Otherwise each completed request was hard when selected and adds at least one new value. The strategy can retain all supplied values, so the stopping condition occurs in finite time for a correct strategy. At stopping, \(n\) completed exits give at most \(n\) one-gate allowances, while each required at least one overhead gate, so \(n\le A\). The available-value count satisfies \(M(d)\le d+n+A\le d+2A\). This proves the claimed ceiling lower bound, including for randomized strategies when the adversary observes realized availability. Its asymptotic order is \(2^{d/2}\). The argument is adaptive and gives no fixed offline instance.

For the fixed prefix example, the feedback realization uses exactly \(d+1\) gates, which is optimal because all \(d+1\) required values are distinct and non-input. A no-feedback cone for the pure sum has all \(d\) source inputs essential. If it has \(g\) binary gates, its connected ancestor graph has at least \(d+g\) vertices and \(2g\) operand edges, giving \(g\ge d-1\). The \(d\) consumer-bearing output gates are distinct and outside that cone. Thus \(2d-1\) is a lower bound, and the clean prefixes plus consumer attachments attain it. The exact full-circuit difference \(d-2\) is linear and independent of the adaptive exponential result.
