# Independent review of tight-fibre wire arguments

2026-11-13. I independently reviewed [TIGHT_FIBRE_WIRES.md](../TIGHT_FIBRE_WIRES.md), concentrating on the adjacent-pair exposure lemma, simultaneous accumulation choices, and the ordinary derivative after deleting trace ports. I found no material gap. The review used symbolic circuit and linear-algebra arguments, with no circuit search or enumeration. This is machine review, not formal verification or human certification.

The count of six XORs in the literal transpose is correct: the original expanded network has nine inputs, four outputs, eleven XOR nodes, and six binary copies. Each of the five formal entry inputs has exactly one reader. Its mirrored output is therefore attached directly to the copy dual to its entry XOR. Deleting these five output ports and suppressing the resulting unary copies gives the precise ordinary projection after reversing again, preserving the relevant original wire positions. It is not an arbitrary second implementation of the same output map.

Bypassing five entries in the original own projection leaves six gates for all four triples. Since six is minimal, none of those gates can be a zero, duplicate, or ordinary input. Each own fibre therefore has one non-entry or input root and only entry edges beyond it. These edges form a rooted tree. The labels on a simple path between two equal-own operands were all read before the exit that uses those operands, including when the path turns at a branching node.

For two adjacent entry edges, the outgoing/outgoing case exposes their trace sum by accumulating those two reader contributions first. In the incoming/outgoing case, the incoming edge's trace is the full adjoint of the common vertex and the outgoing edge contributes the other trace, so all remaining reader and terminal contributions sum to the trace difference. The argument selects the particular accumulator branch carrying that sum. If there is only one remaining contribution, its specific use-wire is selected; the proof does not substitute the full adjoint of every use of its value. Perturbation on that branch reaches the full adjoint accumulator with coefficient one, so its ordinary-output derivative is exactly the original vertex's own mask. Removing formal trace-output branches leaves this ordinary derivative unchanged.

Two adjacent pairs on a three-edge simple path have different internal vertices. Their binary accumulation choices belong to separate original fanout trees and can be made independently in a single literal transpose. This simultaneity is essential: comparing masks exposed only in unrelated transposes would not suffice. The resulting mirror still has six XORs and computes the four ordinary triples. Consequently two distinct nonzero even partial sums must be its two distinct even helper values, rather than extra gate values or zero/duplicate computations.

The even-wire separation proof is valid for those particular wires. In a six-gate triple circuit both helpers are read only by designated odd triple gates, and their reader sets are disjoint. The four triple-output adjoints are independent by their unitriangular terminal contributions. A nonzero derivative on either helper's copy tree is the sum of a nonempty subset of its reader adjoints. Disjoint reader sets therefore exclude equality between a nonzero derivative from one helper and a nonzero derivative from the other. The same conclusion holds after removing any trace-only branches because the selected ordinary derivatives are nonzero.

For three consecutive distinct odd traces, the two exposed adjacent sums are distinct nonzero even masks and both selected wires have the common own-fibre derivative. This contradicts separation. The odd-run theorem follows for paths in either orientation, including paths through a branching node. When all five traces are distinct and odd, every fibre path has at most two edges. Every exit is consequently available or one XOR of earlier actual labels. Chronological source replay then costs no extra gates, including when the labels originally had feedback ancestors. The no-feedback joint minimum of 22 gives the stated lower bound for the all-five-odd class. The note correctly leaves open whether its local consumer trace profile is itself realizable.
