# Independent review: five-label timing

2026-11-13. I checked [FIVE_LABEL_TIMING.md](../FIVE_LABEL_TIMING.md), including its one-addition covering lemma, the prescribed chronological example, and the stated scope. I found no material gap. This is a symbolic verification of the one given example, not a search.

For a rank-four basis `a,b,c,d`, a fifth distinct nonzero value has basis weight two, three, or four. In the weight-two case, supplying `c+d` in addition to `a+b` covers every triple with one of these pairs and covers the four-term sum by their XOR. In the weight-three case, adding `a+d` covers the three missing triples and the four-term sum as stated; basis pairs already cost only one XOR. In the weight-four case, the supplied sum plus a single basis value gives each triple. Thus after all five specified values are physically available, at most one extra XOR makes every space element available or one XOR away. The chronological criterion correctly requires that no preceding exit has already needed additional work; it does not infer early availability from eventual occurrence.

In the fixed example, all partial sums in each consumer chain have nonzero consumer mask, and each final chain pair has the same consumer mask, making exactly the three requested zero-consumer outputs source-side gates. The four initial entries and final entry provide five distinct actual labels of rank four; the fifth arrives only after the three exits. Reusing an identical earlier chain value is a chronological wire reuse. The final output uses the returned value, and all remaining prescribed gates feed one of the designated outputs.

A source-only circuit for the three distinct odd triples needs at least five gates. With at most four, its first gate is an even input pair and the other three gates must be the three required odd triples. No additional even gate is then available, so every derived odd value remains an input or that input XOR the same pair. Only the two inputs outside the pair can yield distinct odd triples, a contradiction. The stated two-pair construction achieves five source gates and produces the triples in the required order, so the source-value-preserving replay overhead relative to the three original exit gates is exactly two.

The scope distinction is necessary and is stated correctly: this is not a separation between optimum full feedback and no-feedback circuits, nor an eleven-gate four-row consumer construction. For the specified functional defining `D`, the returned and subsequently read fifth label is nonclean; its exit pair has consumer mask `z1+z3+z4`, while its later entry pair has mask `z1`, so they are distinct physical pairs. The one-slack collision theorem excludes that pattern in its specialized setting, without affecting the general timing example.
