# Initially free directions can seed chronological group replay

2026-11-14. Structural extension of credited replay. No circuit search or enumeration. The general statement changes the chronological hypothesis; the rank-five consequence strengthens a physical-availability restriction without closing the remaining twelve-consumer case.

## A seeded prefix condition

Use the model of [credited replay](CREDITED_REPLAY.md). Let `U` be the span of the actual consumer entry labels, `E_i` the span of the first `i` entry occurrences, `G_0` the original source gate count, and `A` the original physical source-value set, including all permitted free values. Fix

    U=P direct-sum Q,

and let `S` be a subspace of `Q` whose values are all free from the start. Instead of requiring every `E_i` to split under `P,Q`, assume only

    E_i+S=((E_i+S) intersect P)+((E_i+S) intersect Q)

for every prefix, including `i=0`.

**Seeded replay theorem.** Every original source value and all of `P,Q` can be supplied by a source-only replay with at most

    G_0 + |(P minus {0}) minus A| + |(Q minus {0}) minus A|

gates.

The construction starts with all of `S` physically available, without pretending that the consumer has read it. After a prefix, maintain the entire `P` projection of `E_i` and the entire `Q` space generated by `S` and the `Q` projections of that prefix. The stated split condition says that at a new entry at least one of its two components belongs to the preceding maintained space. Since the actual label is already physically available, the other component can be obtained by one XOR and its enlarged group completed as in ordinary credited replay.

Every exit lies in the span of earlier actual entries, hence in the maintained sum, and uses at most one XOR of the two group components. Ordinary source gates are copied or bypassed chronologically. Each synthetic gate supplies a new nonzero value in `P` or `Q`. If that value belongs to the original physical set `A`, its later original first producer can be bypassed; a producer earlier in the order would already make the value available. Distinct advances charge distinct original producers. Thus only the displayed missing physical values add net cost.

This is the original charging proof with a larger initially supplied group. It neither grants future original values early for free nor changes the actual entry list of the circuit being studied.

## A canonical free-seeded quotient complement

Fix any subspace `D subset U` and any initially free subspace `S subset U` with `D intersect S={0}`. Put

    c=dim(U/D),       s=dim S.

Choose actual entry representatives greedily in chronological order modulo `D+S`. Their number is `c-s`; write them as `n_1,...,n_(c-s)`. Define

    Q=S+span{n_1,...,n_(c-s)}.

Then `U=D direct-sum Q`. Every new selected representative is itself a pure `Q` value. At any unselected occurrence, its `Q` component is a combination of representatives already read and a value of `S`. That component therefore lies in the preceding augmented prefix. Consequently every `E_i+S` splits, and the seeded replay theorem applies with `P=D`.

Writing

    m_D=|A intersect (D minus {0})|,
    m_Q=|A intersect (Q minus {0})|,

the replay cost is at most

    G_0+(2^dim(D)-1-m_D)+(2^c-1-m_Q).

All `2^s-1` nonzero seed values are already free, and the `c-s` selected representatives are distinct original physical values outside the seed. Hence

    m_Q >= (2^s-1)+(c-s).

The role of the seed is causal as well as numerical. An initially free value need not be an actual entry or lie in the span of the actual representatives chosen by the unseeded greedy construction. It can nevertheless be used from time zero in source replay.

## The rank-five free line

Consider the remaining twelve-consumer candidate of total at most 21. The [source parity normal form](EVEN_ENTRY_SOURCE_FLOOR.md) gives `G_0<=9`, an entry space of rank five, and a unique nonzero wholly free value `q`, which is even. It satisfies

    U=D direct-sum span{q,h}

for any odd-own entry `h`. Choose `h` to be the first odd-own actual entry. Greedy selection modulo `D+span{q}` then selects exactly this representative, so the canonical seeded complement is

    Q=span{q,h}={0,q,h,h+q}.

The first two nonzero values `q,h` are original physical values. Thus, with the original physical set `A`,

    m_Q=2+1_{h+q in A}.

The replay supplies all seven nonzero clean diagonals and the source rows in at most

    G_0+8-m_D-1_{h+q in A}

gates. The [fifteen-gate full-diagonal bound](FULL_DIAGONAL_SUPPLY.md) gives the necessary condition

    G_0 >= 7+m_D+1_{h+q in A}.

In particular, if two nonzero clean values are originally physically supplied, then `G_0=9` and **the companion `h+q` of the first odd-own entry is absent from the entire original physical source-value set**. A value supplied later would still count, by advancement credit. This restriction does not require `q` to occur as an actual entry.

The statement concerns the source-value set of the circuit to which the replay is applied. If a free-coordinate normalization is performed first, all physical counts must be recomputed in that transformed circuit. The bound alone leaves zero and one clean value open, and it does not prohibit the two-clean case when this particular companion is absent.
